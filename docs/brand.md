# Visual identity

Open Reason should look like research infrastructure: scientific, technical, open, trustworthy.

- Ink navy `#0B1F33`
- Copper `#C4A35A`
- Paper cream `#F4EFE4`
- Signal teal `#2A6F6F`

The mark is a nested copper-and-teal L with a cream dot at the top, not a robot, brain, or a second letterform.
